# PedsCore v0.1.0-alpha — current public-alpha baseline

Updated: 19 September 2026.

PedsCore is an open-source bilingual pediatric and neonatal clinical-tools project focused on evidence traceability, deterministic calculation where appropriate, explicit rights/reuse handling and conservative safety wording.

Production: https://peds-core.vercel.app/

## Current scope

- **137** cataloged tools/surfaces.
- **134** active surfaces.
- **3** deprecated legacy surfaces.
- **0** active blocked surfaces.
- **0** local-planned entries.
- **64** implemented clinical products.
- **61** local-active calculation surfaces.
- Remaining active non-local tools are official external tools, reference surfaces or workflows where local calculation is not appropriate.
- Rights-limited instruments remain accessible without reproducing protected operational content.

## Major capabilities

- ES/EN web application.
- Clinical Finder and semantic discovery.
- Local deterministic calculators.
- Official/original external references for protected instruments.
- Visible evidence and validation notes.
- WHO and CDC growth workflows.
- Static/prerendered clinical pages.
- Canonical SEO, sitemap/hreflang and IndexNow support.
- Privacy-preserving aggregate analytics.
- No login or clinical-data persistence.

## Clinical safety

PedsCore is educational/informational decision support for qualified professionals. It does not diagnose or prescribe treatment and does not replace clinical judgment, local protocols or institutional policy.

High-risk and prognostic tools are deliberately constrained to descriptive/risk outputs. Clinical values entered in forms are not intended to be persisted or sent to analytics.

## Rights and reuse

Local calculation is allowed only when evidence and reuse gates are compatible. Protected or unresolved instruments can remain active as external reference surfaces. See `docs/CLINICAL_TOOL_RIGHTS.md`.

## Growth

WHO Growth is operational for the implemented WHO 0-5 and 5-19 indicators, including the completed 5-10 weight-for-age range. CDC 2000 percentiles and Extended BMI handling are also active. Third-party data keep their original licensing/attribution conditions.
