## Evidence and limits

Primary source: Gorelick MH, Shaw KN, Murphy KO. Pediatrics 1997;99(5):e6. [Publisher abstract](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/99/5/e6/66343/Validity-and-Reliability-of-Clinical-Signs-in-the), DOI 10.1542/peds.99.5.e6, PMID 9113963; consulted 2026-11-05.

The abstract describes 186 children aged 1 month to 5 years and assessment of ten signs, plus a distinct shortened four-factor version. It supports explicitly distinguishing the variants. This session accessed the abstract, not the full table. It does not establish independent clinical validation, reuse permissions, or new treatment advice. Those limitations remain explicit in the page notes. No new diagnostic performance claims or threshold changes are introduced.
