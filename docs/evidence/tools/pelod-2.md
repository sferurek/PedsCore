## Version
PELOD-2 with the published ten variables, age-specific mean arterial pressure and creatinine thresholds, total score 0-33, and published logistic mortality model.

## Evidence and reuse
Original source:
Leteurtre S, et al. Crit Care Med. 2013.
- DOI: 10.1097/CCM.0b013e31828a2bbd
- PMID: 23685639
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23685639/

The original publication states that PELOD-2 is placed in the public domain. Open-access validation/review literature reproduces the full table and model:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9651518/
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11554295/

## Safety
This is a population-level severity/prognostic score for critical care. It does not provide an individual prognosis guarantee or treatment/escalation recommendation.
