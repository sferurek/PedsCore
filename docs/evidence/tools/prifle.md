# pRIFLE

## PedsCore status
- id: `prifle`
- implementationStatus: `implemented`

## Selected version
Akcan-Arikan pediatric modified RIFLE criteria.

PedsCore classifies by the worst applicable criterion from estimated creatinine-clearance decline and urine output, with Loss/ESRD only when persistent renal-failure duration is explicitly supplied.

## Evidence
Akcan-Arikan A, Zappitelli M, Loftis LL, et al. Kidney Int. 2007;71(10):1028-1035.
- DOI: 10.1038/sj.ki.5002231
- PMID: 17396113
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17396113/

Open-access peer-reviewed review with complete criteria table:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3607497/

## Safety
A reliable baseline eCCl and correct urine-output time window are required. The classification is descriptive and does not generate dialysis, fluid, admission, or medication recommendations.

KDIGO pediatric remains available separately as the more contemporary AKI framework.

`implemented / local_active`
