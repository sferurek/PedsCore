## Selected version
Akcan-Arikan pediatric RIFLE modification.

Classification uses the worst criterion between estimated creatinine-clearance decline and urine output:
- Risk: eCCl decline >=25% or urine output <0.5 mL/kg/h for >=8 h;
- Injury: eCCl decline >=50% or urine output <0.5 mL/kg/h for >=16 h;
- Failure: eCCl decline >=75% or eCCl <35 mL/min/1.73 m2, or urine output <0.3 mL/kg/h for >=24 h, or anuria >=12 h;
- Loss: persistent failure >=4 weeks;
- End-stage: persistent failure >=12 weeks.

## Safety
Requires a defensible baseline eCCl and correct urine-output chronology. It is a staging tool, not a treatment directive.
