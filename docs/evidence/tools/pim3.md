## Status

## Implementation
PIM3 uses the published admission-period variables, coefficients, diagnostic risk groups, missing-data defaults, and logistic conversion to mortality probability.

## Evidence
Straney L, Clements A, Parslow RC, et al. Pediatr Crit Care Med. 2013;14(7):673-681.
- DOI: 10.1097/PCC.0b013e31829760cf
- PMID: 23863821
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23863821/

Open formula/coefficients:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5220006/

Registry documentation also publishes the PIM3 coding rules and coefficients.

## Safety
PIM3 is intended for case-mix, audit, benchmarking and population-level prognostic stratification. It must not be used as an individual treatment or limitation-of-care directive.
