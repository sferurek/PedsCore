## Selected version
Gorelick 10-sign scale for children aged 1 month to 5 years.

Each abnormal sign counts 1 point:
- general appearance;
- capillary refill;
- tears;
- mucous membranes;
- eyes;
- breathing;
- pulse quality;
- skin elasticity;
- heart rate;
- urine output.

Interpretation implemented from the published 10-sign form:
- 0-2: no/minimal dehydration;
- >=3: associated with >=5% body-weight loss;
- >=7: associated with >=10% body-weight loss.

## Safety
The score estimates dehydration severity and does not prescribe oral/IV fluids, admission, discharge, or medication.
