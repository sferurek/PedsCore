# Gorelick Dehydration Scale

## Status
- id: `gorelick_dehydration`
- implementationStatus: `implemented`
- calculationStatus: `active`

## Selected variant
Ten-sign Gorelick scale in children approximately 1 month to 5 years with suspected dehydration.

Each abnormal sign contributes 1 point; total 0-10.

PedsCore uses the published associations:
- 0-2: no/minimal dehydration
- >=3 signs: associated with >=5% dehydration
- >=7 signs: associated with >=10% dehydration

## Evidence
Gorelick MH, Shaw KN, Murphy KO. Pediatrics. 1997;99(5):E6.
- DOI: 10.1542/peds.99.5.e6
- PMID: 9113963
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9113963/

## Safety
The score is descriptive. No oral/IV fluid plan, admission, discharge, or treatment recommendation is generated.
