## Selected version
PedsCore implements the 10-sign Gorelick clinical dehydration scale for children approximately 1 month to 5 years old.

Each abnormal sign contributes one point:
- abnormal general appearance;
- prolonged capillary refill;
- absent tears;
- dry mucous membranes;
- sunken eyes;
- deep breathing;
- weak pulses;
- reduced skin elasticity;
- tachycardia;
- reduced urine output.

Total: 0-10.

## Evidence
Gorelick MH, Shaw KN, Murphy KO. Pediatrics. 1997;99(5):E6.
- DOI: 10.1542/peds.99.5.e6
- PMID: 9113963
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9113963/

Open peer-reviewed reproductions were used to verify the complete 10-sign variant and the published associations:
- 3 or more signs: associated with at least 5% dehydration;
- 7 or more signs: associated with at least 10% dehydration.
