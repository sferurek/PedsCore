## Version
SNAPPE-II (Score for Neonatal Acute Physiology with Perinatal Extension II).

Nine variables:
1. lowest mean blood pressure
2. lowest temperature
3. lowest PaO2/FiO2
4. lowest pH
5. multiple seizures
6. urine output
7. birth weight
8. small for gestational age below the 3rd percentile
9. 5-minute Apgar

Total range: 0-162.

An official US public-health crisis-standards document also reproduces the table for clinical severity stratification.

## Safety
SNAPPE-II is a severity/risk instrument for neonatal populations. PedsCore reports the score only and does not convert it into deterministic individual mortality predictions, treatment decisions, or limitation-of-care criteria.
