# SNAPPE-II

## Status
- implemented / local_active
- nine-variable neonatal severity score
- maximum theoretical score: 162

## Evidence
Open-access scoring table:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4625304/

Variables:
- lowest mean blood pressure
- lowest temperature
- PaO2/FiO2 ratio
- lowest serum pH
- multiple seizures
- urine output
- birth weight
- small for gestational age <3rd percentile
- 5-minute Apgar

## Safety
Uses the published first-12-hour severity construct. PedsCore reports the score only; it does not convert the result into an individual mortality prediction or limitation-of-support decision.
