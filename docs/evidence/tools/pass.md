# PASS — Pediatric Asthma Severity Score

## Status
- id: `pass`
- implementationStatus: `implemented`
- calculationStatus: `active`

## Variant
Gorelick PASS (2004), ages 1-18 years with acute asthma.

Three domains, each 0-2:
1. work of breathing
2. wheezing
3. prolonged expiration

Total: 0-6.

## Evidence
Gorelick MH, Stevens MW, Schultz TR, Scribano PV. Acad Emerg Med. 2004;11(1):10-18.
- DOI: 10.1197/j.aem.2003.07.015
- PMID: 14709423
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14709423/

Open comparative literature reproduces the complete three-domain table:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5425314/

## Safety
PedsCore reports the score only. It does not impose universal treatment bands or medication/disposition recommendations.
